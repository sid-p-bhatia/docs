**Parameta Solution’s** data is stored in Snowflake's industry leading enterprise cloud data warehouse enabling seamless data access to any access service, application or cloud-based infrastructure.

**Snowflake** houses our full inventory of pre-trade, post-trade, indicative/ quote and historic data (going back to 2006\*). Data is available in proprietary Commercial Packages that are split by asset class and currency.

**Parameta’s** data lake has been designed from the ground up to meet our customer’s needs – high availability and immediacy of access to end-of-day, intra-day, and historic pricing across all OTC asset classes.
