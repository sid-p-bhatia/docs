## Setting Up Direct Share

1. **Provide Account Details** — Share your [Snowflake account identifier](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/admin-account-identifier#label-account-name-data-sharing) with your Parameta representative
2. **Accept the Share** — Once shared, accept the share in SnowSight under **Data \> Private Sharing \> Shared With You**
3. **Create Database** — Click **Get Data** to create a database from the share
4. **Start Querying** — Query the data using your preferred tools and workflows


### Follow the below instructions to accept the share
